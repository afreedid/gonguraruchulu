Our Heritage & Passion

Taste The Andhra Soul In Chennai

At Gongura Ruchulu (Dhanam Nagar, Mugalivakkam), we bring the fiery, nostalgic warmth of Telugu kitchens to your plate. Anyone who has tasted genuine Andhra food knows that Gongura (sorrel leaves) is not just an ingredient — it is an emotion.

From our slow-simmered Gongura Dum Biriyani and succulent Mutton Biriyani (₹350) to sizzling Chicken Lollipop, Chicken 555 & Kaju Chicken, every dish is cooked fresh upon order without compromising on authentic spice blends.
