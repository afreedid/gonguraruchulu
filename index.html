Our Heritage & Passion

Taste The Andhra Soul In Chennai

At Gongura Ruchulu (Dhanam Nagar, Mugalivakkam), we bring the fiery, nostalgic warmth of Telugu kitchens to your plate. Anyone who has tasted genuine Andhra food knows that Gongura (sorrel leaves) is not just an ingredient — it is an emotion.

From our slow-simmered Gongura Dum Biriyani and succulent Mutton Biriyani (₹350) to sizzling Chicken Lollipop, Chicken 555 & Kaju Chicken, every dish is cooked fresh upon order without compromising on authentic spice blends.

Ordering & Privacy Policy

Gongura Ruchulu • Mugalivakkam, Chennai

1. Customer Privacy

We respect your privacy. Delivery addresses and phone numbers provided during ordering are stored solely in your local browser for convenient re-orders and transmitted exclusively to Gongura Ruchulu's official WhatsApp manager for delivery execution. We never sell or share your information with third-party advertisers.

2. Order Fulfillment & Delivery

Orders are prepared fresh upon confirmation. Standard delivery time ranges between 25–45 minutes within Mugalivakkam, Porur, Ramapuram, and neighboring areas depending on traffic and kitchen order queues.

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) as well as UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) upon delivery or table checkout. Prices displayed on our menu are accurate and inclusive of applicable preparation costs.
